RIG01 Backups

A private personal backup utility operated by its account owner. It stores encrypted computer backups in a private Google Drive folder and restores them when needed.

The utility uses rclone for transport and restic for encryption and recovery. Google authorization is limited to files and folders created or explicitly opened with this application through the drive.file permission. It also reads the owning account’s storage allowance and usage to avoid filling the account.

This utility has no public registration or hosted backup service. This website contains application information only; it does not provide access to backups, credentials or an account.
