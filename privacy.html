RIG01 Backups privacy policy

Effective October 13, 2026. RIG01 Backups is a personal utility operated solely for its account owner.

Information accessed and purpose

The utility uses Google OAuth to identify the owning account, read storage quota and usage, and create, list, upload, download or delete backup objects in its application-accessible Google Drive folders. Its drive.file authorization does not grant account-wide access to unrelated Drive content. It does not request Gmail, Calendar or Google Photos permissions.

Storage and disclosure

Backup contents and recovery credentials are encrypted locally with restic before upload. OAuth credentials needed for transport are stored locally with restricted Windows file permissions; independently recoverable copies are encrypted. Google receives encrypted backup objects and ordinary storage metadata such as object names, sizes and timestamps. The owner retains private recovery instructions and operational logs.

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Its access and use of Google user data are limited to the backup and recovery functions described here. No separate hosted backup operator receives this data.

Retention, deletion and access control

The owner controls backup retention and deletion. Revoking the application’s Google access stops authorized operations but does not itself delete existing Drive backups. The owner may remove those backups through Google Drive, and may remove locally stored credentials separately. Recovery access should be preserved before deleting the only usable copy.

This website

These static pages add no analytics, tracking scripts or cookies. The hosting provider may maintain its normal server access logs. There is no public signup, form or account access here.

For this single-user utility, privacy requests and configuration are handled by the account owner; the support contact is shown on the Google consent screen.
